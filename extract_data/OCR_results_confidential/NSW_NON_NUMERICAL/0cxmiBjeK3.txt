Physics Stage 6

During this semester students developed their understanding of complex motion by analysing the forces acting on a
system and the associated energy transformations. By applying mathematical techniques, students modeled and
predicted projectile motion and uniform circular motion. They also examined the observations that led to our current
understanding of the nature of light, the development of quantum theory and the theory of relativity. Students explored
the evidence supporting these physical theories and the power of scientific theories have to make predictions.

Japanese Continuers - HSC

The study of Japanese contributes to the overall education of students, particularly in the areas of communication,
cross-cultural understanding, literacy and general knowledge. It promotes understanding of different attitudes and
values within the wider Australian community and beyond and will better equip students as travellers and provide
access to a significant part of the culture, traditions and attitudes of Japanese-speaking communities. In the HSC
course, students focus on three prescribed themes that are the individual, the Japanese speaking communities and
the changing world. Students will also study associated topics and draw on a range of tasks and text types to gain a
broader understanding of the Japanese language and culture while extending and refining their communication skills
in the language.

English Advanced Stage 6
