This report reflects a student’s progress throughout Year 12, marking their final cumulative assessment
results for their Higher School Certificate courses. It is a valuable tool for assessing progress, monitoring
improvement and commenting on effort and application. This report assists a pupil in plotting a course of
study for their final external HSC examinations. Furthermore, it displays a student's measurable level of
dedication as they move beyond secondary schooling.

For this final report, your GPA will be calculated using the achievement grades for English and your next
best eight HSC units. You will also be provided with the average GPA of your cohort (year group).

HSC
Yearly Report 2020

This academic report reflects your child's progress over the course of this year and as such is a valuable
tool in assessing progress as well as monitoring improvement. A report allows students, parents and
teachers the opportunity to discuss and plan a ‘way forward’, a deliberate plan to recognise success and
strive for improvement. Furthermore, this report forms the platform for two unique initiatives in
Barrenjoey’s focus on ‘individualised learning’.

1. The GPA: Your overall achievement grades will generate your current Grade Point Average or GPA.
You will also be provided with the average GPA of your cohort (year group). This acts as a reference point
for comparison with your peers. Over time, the progress of your GPA also allows you to monitor your
academic development.

2. Report feedback: All students will engage in report interviews, allowing them to reflect on their
achievements and to create goals and strategies with senior staff members. These are then recorded for
future use in their individual learning plan. All students from year 7 to 12 follow this important process twice
per year.

The Report and associated initiatives promote a student’s awareness in the learning cycle, ownership of
their results while planning for the future. They are great examples of the level of individual attention we
provide our students at every stage of their school life.
