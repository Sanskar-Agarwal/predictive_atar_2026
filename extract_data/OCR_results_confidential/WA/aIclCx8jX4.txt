These WA school kids are taking their bright
idea to Silicon Valley

Bethany Hiatt
Monday, 24 October 2016 3:20AM

a,

St Mary’s pupils Delaney Eastabrook, Teale Lyon and Kira Molloy are on their way to San Francisco.
They have not even finished school yet, but these Year 11 students are off to Silicon Valley
to pitch an app they created.

The St Mary’s Anglican Girls’ School students designed the app “vocabulary voyagers” to
help primary school students improve their literacy skills by playing games that focus on
vocabulary and spelling.

Teale Lyon, Kira Molloy and Delaney Eastabrook said they were inspired to produce the
app after national NAPLAN results showed little improvement in reading scores.

The girls’ app design won the secondary school category of the Search for Next Tech Girl
Superhero competition run by the not-for-profit Tech Girls Movement.

The students will spend a week visiting technology companies in the US next year and will
pitch their app at the University of San Francisco.

St Mary’s head of technology and enterprise Michelle Fitzpatrick, who coached the girls,
said the free app had already had more than 3000 downloads.

“They had no app development experience 14 months ago,” she said.
Computer coding is a compulsory subject at St Mary’s from kindergarten to Year 10.

Serbia-bound Karrinyup geography
student knows lay of the land

March 7th, 2017, 01:00AM Written by Kate Leaver Stirling Times
NEWS

A St Mary’s Anglican Girls’ School student with a passion for geography will represent
Australia at the 2017 International Geography Olympiad in Serbia this August.

Teale Lyon (17) who was named the top female geography student in WA in the Australian
Geography Competition in December, will travel to the International Olympiad for the best
16 to 19-year-old geography students from all over the world.

The competition consists of three parts; a written test, a multimedia test, and fieldwork with
geographical analysis.

Teale said she was looking forward to the “once in a lifetime experience”.

“lam honoured to be representing Australia in Serbia, | cannot wait to learn and experience
another part of the world,” she said.

“...1am looking forward to Serbia and meeting other geo-minded students from the other 44
different countries.”

The 14th International Geography Olympiad will run from August 2-8.
