Explanation of Generic Criteria:

Punctuality: refers to a student’s ability to get to class regularly and on time. It also infers an
expectation that students are prepared for class (with appropriate resources) when they arrive.

Completion of Set Tasks: refers to student’s evidence of meeting teachers expectations
regarding finishing set work within appropriate time frames and within specified parameters (this
may include quality or quantity guidelines).

AttitudeCommitment: refers to a student’s approach to their learning and their level of
motivation to ensure they are maximising their learning opportunities.

Participation: refers to a student’s level of involvement and engagement in expected learning
activities (e.g. group work, class discussions, brainstorming and collaborative practices).
