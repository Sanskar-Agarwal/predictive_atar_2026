ACADEMIC TRANSCRIPT
Explanatory Notes

The Academic Transcript is provided at least twice per year as notification of a student’s progressive results.
As such, until the final result of Year 12 is determined, all subject results in all year levels are provided as “working towards”.

Most subjects are reported with numerical results. On the transcript you will see...

e Subject Result is the student’s progressive overall achievement in summative assessment tasks thus far in the course shown as a
percentage result.

oO. Year 12 results: the Year 12 system involves a Confirmation process conducted by QCAA through which the school’s marking is reviewed to
confirm results. The numerical result on this transcript may, therefore, be a combination of Confirmed (final) and Provisional (yet to be
confirmed) results. Further details are available from the class teacher.

e Distribution of Subject Results identifies how many students at the time of this transcript are placed in each result range. Note that
each percentage band corresponds with a descriptor that is used by the College to indicate progress against national and state
curriculum standards:

Emerging Developing Demonstrating Advancing Extending
0% - 29% 30% - 54% 55% - 72% 73% - 87% 88% - 100%

Some subjects in Years 10 to 12 are reported with letter-grade results. On the transcript you will see...

e Subject Result will be shown as a letter grade from A to E (without +/- qualifiers); this system is used for Applied-introductory and
Applied level subjects in Years 10 and 11.

e Distribution of Subject Results: the distribution range corresponds to subject-specific standards for A to E judgements; details about
a subject’s A to E standards are available from the class teacher, Head of Faculty and online subject syllabus.

Other points to note

e Subjects not appearing on the transcript: if a student has no summative result available in a subject, the subject will not appear on
the transcript. Non-assessed subjects (e.g. CLT, Study) are not reported.

e Modified: students who have undertaken modified curriculum and assessment will have those subjects identified on the transcript by
the word “Modified” appearing underneath the name of the subject. Results in modified courses are not directly comparable with
results for non-modified courses/peers.

e Other feedback: further detailed feedback on formative and summative tasks is available to students and parents through Canvas,
returned assessment items and directly from the class teacher.

e §=Co-curricular: the transcript also provides a list of the student’s co-curricular activities to date in the current school year.

e IMPACT OF COVID-19: due to the disruption of the COVID pandemic, a number of subjects were unable to finish summative
assessment in Term 1, and so results may reflect amended assessment programs; students and parents have access to other
formative feedback through Canvas and by direct contact with the class teacher. Co-curricular activity listings have also been
impacted and so may not appear.

Other details about the transcript and student progress are available from Canvas, class teachers, Heads of Faculty, Academic Docs,
and the Director of Studies.
