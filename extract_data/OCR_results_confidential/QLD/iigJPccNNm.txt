Liam is an unassuming, personable and considerate young man who has demonstrated his capacity for
perseverance and excellence over this time. As a teacher, it has been gratifying to watch him develop
from earning Bs consistently through Year 11 to earning As in every task in Year 12. That his results have
continued to improve across each task reflect his willingness to act on feedback and manage his time
well. As a class member, he is friendly and endearing, generously contributing to class discussion to
develop his own understanding of the subject matter without overshadowing his peers. That his group of
friends in the class have also consistently improved in their results is partly a result of his supportiveness
and generosity of dialogue. This excellence is reflected in his recent enrolment in the subject of Modern
History, where he has achieved at an even higher standard of A, despite only having studied the subject
for a year.

improvement in his performance in Specialist Maths and Biology from Year 11 into 12 as he has with his
English. He is genuinely interested and motivated to work in these subject areas, and would be an
