Explanatory Notes

Your daughter's Academic Progress Report presents Queensland Curriculum and Assessment Authority’s (QCAA)
confirmed results for QCAA Assessment Event 1 summative tasks completed during Term 4 2020 and Term 1
2021.

These reported results are the first of four task results in each subject. These task results are used to determine a
student’s overall grade for each subject upon completion of Year 12. The four assessment tasks consist of two
internal assessments and one external assessment.

Detailed task-specific comments for each piece of completed assessment have been made available during the
semester via the School’s Continual Reporting platform. Parents can access the platform via Minerva or the BGGS
App. Parents and students are encouraged to review this feedback in preparation for further assessment tasks during
2021.
