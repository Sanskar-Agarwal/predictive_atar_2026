Sean has been a student at Marist College North Shore since 2014. During his time at the College he
has made a significant contribution to the College community. In recognition of his contribution to
the pursuit of learning and academics as well as for his diligent attitude and willingness to
participate in all aspects of College life, Sean was voted by his peers and teachers to the position
of Academic Captain.

Sean is a dedicated and passionate learner with a strong ethic in self-improvement. He has an
open mind and an empathetic appreciation of new ideas and perspectives, with a strength in
critically deconstructing confronting issues to better his own understanding and to help others
understand them. His succinct articulation of ideas and issues is advantageous to the smooth
functioning of groups he is involved in. He is a highly respected member of his year group. In 2017
Sean was awarded the esteemed Junior Champagnat Award for his outstanding contributions to
College life and for most exemplifying the qualities of St Marcellin Champagnat.

Sean has consistently achieved outstanding academic success in his subjects over the years. He
was Dux of his year group in 2014, 2015, 2016 and 2017. In Year 12 he received Academic Excellence
Awards in Modern History, Physics and Studies of Religion. Further to this, he was awarded the
‘Senior Proficiency Award” as the student with significant contribution to the life of the College.
Sean is highly organised, thorough, and conscientious, with sound time management skills, all of
which has contributed to his outstanding academic success.

Sean’s commitment to serving the community (both the School and the broader community) has
been evident through his involvement in social justice activities. He travelled to Cambodia in the
school holidays to work with the less fortunate and assisted in education programs in local
communities. He was also a member of the College’s Solidarity program, where he participated in
the St Vincent de Paul Society’s Night Patrol, and assisted at the Matthew Talbot Hostel serving the
homeless of Sydney. Sean was also Peer Support leader in 2018 where he mentored Year Seven
students in their transition into the College. At all times Sean was an outstanding ambassador for
the College.

Sean’s well rounded nature was also shown in his representing the school in Cultural pursuits. He
has outstanding performance and musical skills which he shared with the community at the annual
College musicals and performance nights. Sean is also an accomplished Chess player and he
represented the College on multiple occasions regularly reaching the finals of the Metropolitan
Secondary Schools Chess Competition.

His participation in representing the College in sporting pursuits in the past six years was
an admirable aspect of Sean’s contribution to the College. He was the Vice Captain of the
A Grade Cricket team representing the College across all of his years. He was also a
member of the B Grade Football team and the MCC Cross Country Team. His involvement
in representative sport reflects his commitment to training, leadership qualities, working
within a team and self-discipline.

Sean has a mature outlook on life and his studies; he is highly capable, honest, trustworthy,
respectful and empathetic. He is a fine role model and an honest friend for many. He is a
thoughtful young man who is always friendly and courteous. Sean has a strong sense of school
spirit and loyalty, is willing to take on responsibilities and has a diligent and collaborative approach
to work. His likeable personality has endeared him to the staff and his peers. He has the ability to
work under pressure and is highly self-motivated. Sean was always punctual, respectful,
trustworthy and empathetic.

We have no hesitation in recommending Sean to you and please do not hesitate to contact the
College should you require further information.
