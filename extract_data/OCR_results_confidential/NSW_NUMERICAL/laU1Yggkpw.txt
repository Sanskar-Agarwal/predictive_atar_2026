12 Mathematics Extension 1 (HSC)

The calculus-based Mathematics Extension 1 and Mathematics Extension 2 courses are designed
to promote the development of knowledge, skills and understanding in relation to important
concepts within areas of Mathematics that have applications in an increasing number of contexts.
This includes the development of deductive and inductive reasoning skills and the ability to solve
challenging mathematical problems via the use and synthesis of course theorems. For an overall
comment about Mark's progress in both courses please refer to the comment in the Mathematics
Extension 2 section of this report.

A typical student who achieves Academic Honours in Stage 6 Mathematics Extension 1,
demonstrates extensive knowledge, skills and understanding appropriate to the course and uses
sophisticated multi-step reasoning in both numerical and theoretical contexts. He rapidly
integrates ideas from the Mathematics course using well-developed algebraic, deductive or
graphing skills to successfully solve a variety of familiar and unfamiliar problems. He also
communicates effectively and efficiently, appropriate mathematical language, notation, diagrams
and graphs.
