ENGLISH ADVANCED Mr J Tod-Hill

Students studied George Orwell's ‘Nineteen Eighty-Four’ in the unit, ‘Texts and Human Experiences’. They
explored how texts shape and are shaped by varied human experiences throughout time. They chose a
related text and wrote an essay that examined how authors responded to human experiences. Then
students engaged in a comparative study of Shakespeare's ‘King Richard Ill’ and Pacino's ‘Looking for
Richard’. They examined how form, context, purpose and values shape meaning in different texts.

MATHEMATICS EXTENSION 1 Mr P Rundle

Students extended their study of probability into permutations and combinations. They studied the binomial
theorem and rates of change. Students discovered the method of induction proofs which can prove results
for an infinite number of cases. They investigated trigonometry as a function, the associated calculus, as
well as compound angles and t-results. Students graphed sine waves, rational functions and graphs with
oblique asymptotes and observed the symmetry of inverse trigonometric curves.
