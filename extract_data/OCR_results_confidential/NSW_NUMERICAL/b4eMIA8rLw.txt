To Whom It May Concern,

Phoebe Hendriks is currently a student at Model Farms High School, completing her
Higher School Certificate as a Year 12 candidate in 2021.

Phoebe enrolled into Model Farms High School in 2016 and has been an outstanding
student and ambassador for the school throughout this time. Phoebe is an energetic
and cheerful young lady, with a wonderfully vibrant personality. She is honest,
reliable and has a keen sense of humour, relating particularly well to adults and her
peers. Her positive outlook and discretion make her a person whose opinion is
always highly valued.

She is also an outstanding academic student, being Dux of her Year group in 2016,
2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020. Since 2016, Phoebe has gained the first position on her
yearly reports in courses on 25 occasions including the sciences, mathematics,
English courses, humanities and creative arts.

Phoebe is currently a member of the school’s Senior Leadership Team through her
position of school Vice-Captain. Phoebe gained this position through a merit process,
recognised by both staff at the school and her peers. In this role, Phoebe volunteers
countless hours for the betterment of the student cohort and the school as a whole.
Her contributions are always thoughtful, analytical and considers the needs of
others.

Phoebe’s attendance throughout her time at the school has been outstanding,
demonstrating her commitment to her education. Since 2016, Phoebe has averaged
an attendance of 98.1%.

developing the skills of the school’s future leaders. It is incredible to think that Phoebe has developed a working
ethos (complete with proformas) that will act as an invaluable resource for future generations of school leaders.

Of course, as well as Phoebe’s outstanding intellectual capacity — she matches this wonderful academic talent, with

calm nature, her ability to transcend age groups (staff and students) is a leadership skill and prowess that can’t be

always learn are skills she will inevitably carry with her into the next stage of her journey. In troubled times, when
our notions of leadership are being challenged, | have complete faith that Phoebe will continue to lead in a diverse
and exciting array of new ventures. The world awaits her.
