Reporting Student Achievement in Year 12

The School provides parents and caregivers with ongoing information and guidance on each student’s
growth in scholarship, personal development and citizenship. Such information is shared in several ways.

Information Evenings
Parents and students are invited to attend a function where senior teachers explain specific issues and
plans.

Parent, Student and Teacher Interviews
Academic interviews provide an opportunity for parents to discuss their child’s achievement and
application with each of their teachers.

Parent, Student and Mentor meetings
An opportunity for students and parents to meet with the Mentor and set goals for the year ahead.

Feedback on Assessment Tasks
Continuous reporting of progress on assessment tasks through the HG Engage App.

Semester Reports
Every semester a formal written report is provided to parents summarising a student’s achievement and
application in each subject.

How to Read this Academic Report

The Academic Report presents a comprehensive summary of a student’s achievement and application for
each course they are enrolled in. The information provided will normally include:

* progress on course outcomes

* an indicator of overall effort

* an overall A to E grade for each course (Years 1-11)

* asummary of results in the formal assessment program

* approaches to learning in class and home learning (half yearly report)

* teacher’s comment, including areas for focus leading into final HSC Examinations (yearly report)

Reported Marks

Our formal assessment program includes between 3 and 4 assessments per subject for Year 12 and we
report the individual results for each assessment. At the time of the half yearly report, either 1 or 2
assessment marks will be reported (AT1, AT2). The yearly report will include all completed assessment
tasks, and an overall course mark, expressed as a percentage. The yearly report will include additional
comparative data, such as course average and mark distribution.

Approaches to Learning

Contemporary education places great emphasis on the importance of the student’s own role in learning.
Rather than learning being a process where the teacher 'delivers' knowledge to the student, the School
sees teachers and students engaged in a co-operative partnership where together they work to develop
the student’s understanding. We urge students to be active learners and to reflect on all the ways in
which they are involved in learning in lessons and at home. The level of involvement and commitment are
reported in the Approach to Learning. The descriptors used to define a student’s Approach to Learning as
indicated in this report are Consistently, Frequently, Sometimes and Rarely.

Teacher Analysis

After each assessment task, teachers will write a comment on student progress, published in HG Engage.
In addition, the yearly report will include a summary of yearly examination performance and an overall
comment on current strengths and areas for improvement. Students and their parents are encouraged
to seek further feedback as required at the Student-Teacher-Parent Interviews and on other occasions
throughout the year.
