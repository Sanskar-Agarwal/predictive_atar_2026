Senior Report Information

Our school reports your child’s progress with written reports twice a year and provides ongoing detail through
interviews or meetings. Please contact the school to discuss this report and participate in your child’s learning and
progress with the classroom teacher.

Reports for TAFE subjects will be issued separately by TAFE. Reports for VET subjects will be issued by St Joseph
Trades Skills Centre.

Explanation of grading in courses (except VET)

A - Extensive: The student has an extensive knowledge and understanding of the course content for this
particular stage of the course, and can readily apply this knowledge within various contexts.
This student has displayed a high level of competence in the processes and skills for this
stage of the course and can apply these skills to new situations.

B - Thorough: The student has a thorough knowledge and understanding of the course content for this
particular stage of the course, and has displayed a high level of competence in the processes
and skills specific to this stage of the course. In addition the student is able to apply this
knowledge and these skills to new situations.

C - Sound: The student has a sound knowledge and understanding of the main areas of the course
content and has displayed an adequate level of competence in the processes and skills at this
stage of the course.

D - Basic: The student has a basic knowledge and understanding of the course content and has
displayed a limited level of competence in the processes and skills at this stage of the course.

E - Elementary: The student has an elementary knowledge and understanding in a few areas of the content
and has displayed very limited competence in the processes and skills at this stage of the
course.

N - Not evident: The student has not been able to meet the standard for this outcome/area for assessment.

Learning Outcomes
The Senior Report indicates student performance in their Trial HSC Examination and Final Course Assessment Rank.

Trial HSC Examination Mark
The Examination Mark, relates to the mark attained by the student in the Half Yearly Examination this year.

Trial HSC Examination Rank
The Examination Rank reflects where each student is ranked against other students in the Half Yearly Examination.

Final HSC Course Rank

The Final Course Rank is the ranking of the student after each formal assessment task and in accordance with the
weighting of each task as outlined in the assessment schedules. This ranking may change following each assessment
task, or as a result of illness / misadventure appeals as decided by the school assessment committee.

It is important that students reflect on their performance and discuss areas for improvement/development with their
classroom teacher.
