OVERALL INDICATIVE GRADES

Each subject reports student achievement which is represented by a grade ranging from A(highest) to E(lowest). The grades
represent the indicative achievement in a subject for Units 1 & 2 and are based on the completed internal assessment items.
At the end of this Report is a graph representing the calculated academic achievement of your son for every semester of
study based on the grades given for each subject. The calculated academic achievement shown in this graph is based on:

(a) The year level your son is in. The A to E grades given for subjects have different numerical values which are determined
by the year level your son was in for each semester. This is to reflect the progressively increasing academic challenges
your son encounters from one year to the next.

(b) The number of contact periods each subject occupies in the weekly academic programme. For example, a subject like
Mathematics, taught for nine periods per cycle, contributes more to the calculated academic achievement than a subject
taught for less periods.

Application refers to a student’s approach to learning and the level of application demonstrated in the subject.

subject application ratings are then averaged to produce a single number, the Overall Subject Application Rating
(OSAR). The OSAR serves a similar function to the Grade Point Average (GPA). Comparisons should be made
between a student's GPA and OSAR when considering strategies to improve GPA outcomes.
