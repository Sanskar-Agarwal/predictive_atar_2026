Level of Achievement
Achievement Achievement Description

Outstanding The student has an extensive knowledge and understanding of the content and can readily apply this
knowledge. In addition, the student has achieved very high levels of competence in the processes and
skills and can apply these skills to new situations.

High The student has a thorough knowledge and understanding of the content and a high level of competence
in the processes and skills and can apply these skills to most situations.

Sound The student has a sound knowledge and understanding of the main areas of content and has achieved an
adequate level of competence in the processes and skills.

Basic The student has a basic knowledge and understanding of the main areas of content and has achieved a
limited level of competence in the processes and skills.

Limited The student has an elementary knowledge and understanding in a few areas of content and has achieved
a very limited level of competence in some of the processes and skills.

NBSC Manly Campus can provide written information, following parent application, showing your child's
achievement compared to his or her peer group at our school. This information shows the number of students in
each of the five achievement levels based on the "Overall Level of Achievement" for each subject.
