Subject: Society and Culture Teacher: Ms N. Saab

This semester in Society and Culture saw students continue their exploration of Popular Culture. Students focused on
the case study of Comic Book Films and explored how this once sub-culture emerged into a global popular culture.
They developed an understanding of how key processes such as commercialisation and globalisation have fostered
the growth of popular cultures. Students have also explored a further Depth Study, that of Belief Systems and
Ideologies. Students gained an understanding of the nature of religious and non-religious belief systems and
ideologies. Students have also explored Buddhism as a focus study during this unit. Students were able to explore the
key changes and continuities Buddhism as a belief system has experienced throughout time.
