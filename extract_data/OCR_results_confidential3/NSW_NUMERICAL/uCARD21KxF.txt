Below is an explanation of the information presented in the Preliminary HSC Course Academic Report. If you have any further
questions regarding the information presented, please contact the College.

Course Mark Graphic

This graphic gives information regarding the student's course mark, course average and relative position within the course for the
Preliminary HSC Course. In the example below:

+ The student's Preliminary HSC course mark is 68 (represented by the number)
+ The Preliminary HSC course average is 62 (represented by the solid black circle)

+ The highest Preliminary HSC course mark awarded was 95 (represented by the right edge of the solid red bar)
+ The lowest Preliminary HSC course mark awarded was 19 (represented by the left edge of the solid red bar).
