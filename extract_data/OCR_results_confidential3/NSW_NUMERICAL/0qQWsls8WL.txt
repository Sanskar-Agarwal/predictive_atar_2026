Course Outline

Physics involves the study of radiation and matter by considering the concepts of energy and force. This semester
students explore how experimental evidence leads to the development of new scientific theories. They study a
variety of theories of light including Einstein's Theory of Special Relativity and the Quantum Model of Light. Then
they delve into the structure of the atom, from the electron to the quark, analysing a number of key discoveries and
the experiments behind them. This leads them into gaining an understanding of our current model of the universe
and the evidence we have to support it.
