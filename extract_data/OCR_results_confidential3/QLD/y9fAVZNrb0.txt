EXPLANATORY NOTES

Overall Result: The overall progress result for each subject is calculated using all available
results up to this point. Most results will be shown as an A* to E- grading. Some subjects,
including Vocational Education subjects, use Satisfactory/Unsatisfactory instead. For Years 7
to 9, a Middle Years Program (MYP) progress result will also be included.

Subject Criteria: Achievement criteria may vary between subjects. They reflect the specific
dimensions of each course that are formally assessed.

Grading Descriptors: Grades awarded indicate the achievement of the student against the
criteria described above.

Application Criteria: All teachers are required to report on three criteria that reflect the
student's application in each subject. These criteria are not formally assessed and hence do
not contribute to the overall result.

e Behaviour: The extent to which the student’s behaviour contributes to a positive

learning environment for themselves and others in the class.

Engagement with Learning: The extent to which the student engages with the
subject matter, participates in class activities and completes assigned tasks in
lessons.

Preparedness for Learning: The extent to which the student completes homework
assignments and comes to class prepared with the necessary equipment and
materials and a positive attitude.

Middle Years Program (MYP) Result Scale (Years 7 to 9)

Grade 1

Minimal achievement in terms of the objectives

Grade 2

Very limited achievement against all the objectives. The student has difficulty in
understanding the required knowledge and skills and is unable to apply them fully in
normal situations, even with support.

Grade 3

Limited achievement against most of the objectives, or clear difficulties in some
areas. The student demonstrates a limited understanding of the required knowledge
and skills and is only able to apply them fully in normal situations with support.

Grade 4

A good general understanding of the required knowledge and skills, and the ability to
apply them effectively in normal situations. There is occasional evidence of the skills
of analysis, synthesis and evaluation.

Grade 5

A consistent and thorough understanding of the required knowledge and skills, and
the ability to apply them effectively in a variety of situations. The student generally
shows evidence of analysis, synthesis and evaluation where appropriate and
occasionally demonstrates originality and insight.

Grade 6

A consistent and thorough understanding of the required knowledge and skills, and
the ability to apply them in a wide variety of situations. Consistent evidence of
analysis, synthesis and evaluation is shown where appropriate. The student generally
demonstrates originality and insight.

Grade 7

A consistent and thorough understanding of the required knowledge and skills, and
the ability to apply them almost faultlessly in a wide variety of situations. Consistent
evidence of analysis, synthesis and evaluation is shown where appropriate. The
student consistently demonstrates originality and insight and always produces work
of high quality.
