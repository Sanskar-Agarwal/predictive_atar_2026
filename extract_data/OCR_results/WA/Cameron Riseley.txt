Cameron Riseley Year 12 WACE Report

Cameron Semester 1, 2017

Chemistry - ATAR Grade A

Examination Mark 86 Course Mark 80
Examination Average 71 Course Average 68

Cameron's performance in the multiple-choice section of the examination was very encouraging. In the short-
answer section of the examination, which tests knowledge rather like the multiple-choice section, but without
the answers being there to see, Cameron did impressively well in all topics. Having said that, he would benefit
from practising questions that ask him to describe observations in chemical tests. In the final section of the
examination, where students are required to provide longer written answers and solve multiple-step
calculations, Cameron performed a satisfactory understanding of the topics covered and an ability to express
himself clearly in his mathematical working. He would benefit from setting out his explanations more clearly
and perhaps using dot points to avoid the risk of contradicting himself or wandering off the point. On the
whole, Cameron's examination performance was extremely pleasing and I hope he will be able to maintain his
form this semester. More generally, Cameron has had an excellent first semester in Chemistry. He can always
be relied upon to have his notes ready at the start of a week. In order to improve further Cameron should focus
on improving the organisation of his notes. A longer-term view towards his work will be of huge benefit to him
now and when he leaves school. Just because something is understood in the short term does not guarantee
that it will appear simple after a period of not using that knowledge. He is more than welcome to meet with me
outside of class, be that before or after school, as he approaches the final stages of the course. It has been a
privilege to have a young man with Cameron's eye for detail in the class and the way he has conducted himself
has been a great credit to him. It is great to see him achieving scores that reflect his ability and I hope that he

will continue to raise the bar in the final few months of the course.

Mr T P Iwanowski

English - ATAR Grade A

Examination Mark 73 Course Mark 78
Examination Average 60 Course Average 63

Cameron is a bright and self-motivated student who has demonstrated an excellent understanding of English
this semester. He has made pleasing progress with the clarity and conciseness of his written expression, as well
as in developing the depth of his technical analyses. Continued focus in this area, specifically on the
conventions of genre, should assist Cameron within his narrative and persuasive compositions. Within the
examination Cameron expressed himself clearly and displayed his strong technical analysis skills. He should

aim to provide greater detail and balance on all requirements of the questions.
