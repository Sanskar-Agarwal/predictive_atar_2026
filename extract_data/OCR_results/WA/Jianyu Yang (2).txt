Information for parents of students

This report is a summary of your child's progress and achievement across his academic program in Semester 1.
In preparing the report, staff have sought to be objective and give a clear, honest, and precise assessment.

Most reports contain a diagnostic element, suggesting areas of focus for student guidance and attention. Parents
are encouraged to use this report as a basis for discussion with their sons on their performance to date and to
celebrate achievement of academic goals as well as identifing areas for further improvement. This may call for
changed expectations, changed attitudes, improved study habits, resetting goals, or a combination of these.

School reports should always be interpreted with a view to the future. Students should be encouraged towards
personal excellence and the maintenance of grades consistent with their ability and academic potential.
