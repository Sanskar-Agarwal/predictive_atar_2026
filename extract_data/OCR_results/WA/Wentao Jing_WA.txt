Explanatory Notes

The information contained in this report is a summary of your child's achievement, attitude,
behaviour and effort. Formal reports are provided twice a year at the end of each school
semester. This report is one of a number of strategies used by the school to communicate to
you about your child's progress.

Course Unit Results

The WACE course units are semester or year length and organised into ATAR and General
courses which represents the degree of difficulty and complexity. ATAR course units are the
more difficult of the course units.

1. Grades represent student achievement in relation to the difficulty of the unit.

Therefore a B grade in an ATAR unit represents a higher achievement than a
General course unit.

2. Marks (if included) for each Course will be a progress mark out of 100.

Endorsed Programs

Endorsed programs of varying duration are undertaken within or outside of school time in
workplace learning (ADWPL), VET stand-alone, university studies, community organisation and
personal development.

Students are awarded a result that indicates the level of achievement within the context of
the program.

Grades

Grades can be interpreted as follows:
A - Excellent achievement

B - High achievement

C - Satisfactory achievement

D - Limited achievement

E - Unsatisfactory achievement

U - Requirements not completed

Online Literacy and Numeracy Assessment (OLNA) Results

In order to achieve the Western Australian Certificate of Education (WACE), students are required to demonstrate that they have met a minimum standard in
literacy (reading and writing) and numeracy. Students can meet these minimum standards either by achieving Band 8 or higher in the NAPLAN reading, writing
and numeracy tests in Year 9 or by meeting the minimum standards of the reading, writing and numeracy components of the Online Literacy and Numeracy
Assessment (OLNA) from Year 10. Students have up to six opportunities (two per year) from Year 10 through to Year 12 to demonstrate the literacy and
numeracy minimum standards.
