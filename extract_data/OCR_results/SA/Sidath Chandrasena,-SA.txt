As you know, we believe that developing the 4 Cs — Creativity, Critical Thinking, Collaboration and Communication,
is-a critical aspect of preparing students for the 21% Century. In previous Semester One reports subject teachers
have reported on the progress of your daughter/son in the 4 Cs.

We have moved to a different process in 2019. While we have strengthened our emphasis on developing the 4 Cs,
we will not report on them at the end of Semester One. Instead, students will present their progress in the 4Cs at
a student/family/teacher conference at the beginning of Term 4.

In preparation for this presentation, students are working with their subject teachers to evaluate their level of skill
in the 4 Cs, against continuums. They will use these continuums to further develop their skills during the year. This
change represents a shift to students taking greater ownership of their learning, (with teacher support) and
enables them to track the growth in their skill development. It also is the beginning of creating a digital presence
with evidence of skills and understandings that students can use for entrance to further study and employment.
