range:

Comparative Reporting

The graphs on the report card show your child’s level of
achievement in each subject against the achievements of

undertook the subject. This information will show you the
number of students in each of the levels of achievement.
This information is subject to the privacy of individual
students being maintained (as a result some graphs may
