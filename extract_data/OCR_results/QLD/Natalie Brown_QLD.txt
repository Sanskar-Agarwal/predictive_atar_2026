sometimes prepared, punctual and respectful of the learning environment.

completes a few tasks to the best of her ability.

Effort Rating 1

The student rarely participates actively in class activities. She is rarely
prepared, punctual and respectful of the learning environment. The student
does not work consistently to improve her skills and understanding. She rarely
completes tasks to the best of her ability.

12 July 2022

To Whom It May Concern

NATALIE BROWN has been a student at St Hilda's School from 24 January 2017 when
she commenced in Year 7. On Friday, 18 November 2022, Natalie will graduate as a Year
12 student. Natalie has been a member of my Pastoral Care class during this time, so I
am in a confident position to attest to the excellent qualities of this young woman.

A sincere, confident young woman, Natalie has enjoyed very productive relationships
with students across year groups and the staff of the School. We have appreciated
Natalie’s willingness to support all School expectations as well as give so generously of
her time to our community. These qualities were recognised with Natalie’s selection as a
Student Ambassador in Year 10, and Prefect with the Portfolio of Academic and Creative
Ideas in Year 12.

A committed and capable student, Natalie has achieved very pleasing results in all
academic subjects. Natalie’s teachers commend her diligent approach to her learning,
her ability to consolidate her skills, and her commitment to achieving her personal best.
In her time in the Senior School, Natalie has been awarded Academic Achievement
Awards, Academic Honours, a Principal’s Award and the STEM Scholarship Award.

Notwithstanding the impact of COVID-19 restrictions during her Senior years, and the
demands of Year 12, Natalie has been actively involved in the co-curricular life of the
School. She has volunteered as a mentor with the Junior School Big Sister Reading and
Maths Program, participated and supported the efforts of her peers in House sporting
events and cultural activities, and represented the School in QGSSSA Netball for a
number of years. Natalie has additionally completed studies in Speech and Drama with
Trinity College London to Level 5 with Distinction. Natalie has been a most excellent
role model for younger students and for her peers.

I am delighted to recommend Natalie as a very hardworking, responsible and considerate
young woman. I am sure she will continue to develop her talents and be a positive
influence in whatever she may choose to follow in life. I wish her every success in her
future endeavours.
