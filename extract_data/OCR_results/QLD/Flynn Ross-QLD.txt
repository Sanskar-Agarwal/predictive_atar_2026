ACADEMIC TRANSCRIPT
Explanatory Notes

The Academic Transcript is provided twice per year as notification of a student’s progressive results. As such, until the final
result of Year 12 is determined, all Subject Results should be understood as “working towards”.

Further detailed feedback on formative and summative tasks is available to students and parents through Canvas.

The transcript also provides a list of the student’s co-curricular activities to date in the current school year.

Years 7 to 11
Subjects reported with numerical results

e Subject Result is the student’s progressive overall achievement in summative assessment tasks thus far in the course shown as a
percentage result.

e Distribution of Subject Results identifies how many students at the time of this transcript have achieved in each result range.
Note that each percentage band corresponds with a descriptor that is used by the College to indicate progress against national
and state curriculum standards:

Emerging Developing Demonstrating Advancing Extending
0% - 29% 30% - 54% 55% - 72% 73% - 87% 88% - 100%

Subjects reported with letter grades

e Subject Result will be shown as a letter grade from A to E (without +/- qualifiers); this system is used for a few Applied-
introductory and Applied level subjects in Years 10 and 11.

e Distribution of Subject Results: the distribution range corresponds to subject-specific standards for A to E judgements; details
about a subject’s A to E standards are available from the class teacher, Head of Faculty and online subject syllabus.

Year 12

e Subject Result: as the last OP cohort, each student’s subject result, or LOA (Level of Achievement), continues to be reported ona
VHA to VLA 15-point scale using +/- qualifiers i.e. from VHA+ to VLA-

e Distribution of Subject Results: the 5-band distribution range for Year 12 results uses VHA to VLA:

VHA - Very High Achievement
HA - High Achievement

SA - Satisfactory Achievement
LA - Limited Achievement

VLA - Very Limited Achievement
