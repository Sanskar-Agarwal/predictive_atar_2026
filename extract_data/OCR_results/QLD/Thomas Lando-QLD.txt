To Whom It May Concern

Thomas Lando

I have known Thomas Lando for the last two years in my capacity as his Senior Physics
teacher. Thomas is an enthusiastic, inquisitive student who is passionate about Physics,
Aerospace Studies and Mathematics. His Senior College subjects are Mathematics B,
Mathematics C, Physics, Chemistry, Aerospace Studies, English and Certificate III in
Christian Ministry & Theology. Thomas has a strong desire to commence tertiary studies
in engineering.

This year, Thomas has demonstrated great interest, proficiency and ability in all aspects
of the Physics course, achieving a very high VHA level. He is a logical, methodical
thinker and problem solver. Thomas readily draws on a wide variety of strategies and
techniques when tackling a problem. His success not only in Physics, but also in all the
other subjects is attributed to his industrious, conscientious attitude and analytical skills
of the highest order. Over the last two years, Thomas has participated in ICAS, ANCQ,
CQU schools Titration and McDonalds’ Maths competitions. Last year, he achieved an
Academic Excellence Award and an Academic Effort Award. Thomas is a highly
motivated and strongly principled young man, who will give a complete effort to
anything he undertakes.

Thomas is a highly intelligent, capable and adaptable student who performs very well
under pressure and displays excellent leadership qualities when working as a team
member. This year, he has been one of the four College Captains of St Patrick’s College,
leading the College assemblies and being a great role model to other students.

Thomas actively supports the College ethos, both religiously and academically. He has
been involved in Music Ministry and has been a frequent participant of the Saturday
School program for the last two years. Thomas actively seeks opportunities to extend his
knowledge and this year he took part in Lions Youth of the Year program and Australian
Youth Aerospace Forum.

Socially, I perceive Thomas as a considerate, polite and likeable young man with
impeccable manners. His dress and grooming have been excellent.

Thomas is a well-balanced student who has the ability to juggle studies and social
commitments. He is an accomplished hockey and rugby league player. For the last two
years, he has been a volunteer umpire at hockey. Thomas is a vice-captain of Under 18’s
Mackay Representative Hockey Team. In 2017 he received the Norths Hockey Club A2
Men’s Best Team Player award and 2017 Norths Hockey Club Clifford Denney

I wish Thomas all the best. He is a young man with a great future who will be successful

I have no hesitation in recommending Thomas for university and residential college
