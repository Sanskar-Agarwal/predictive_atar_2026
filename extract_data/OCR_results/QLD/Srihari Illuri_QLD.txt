Student Report Summary

Levels of achievement for IB Diploma Programme group subjects are reported as 1 - 7 and represent a student's progress in that subject to
date. The Inner Core subject, Theory of Knowledge progress is reported A — E and the Extended Essay progress is reported as S or U.
Details describing these levels of achievement are included below.

A student's Grade Point Average (GPA), total Higher Level (HL) points and total Standard Level (SL) points are included. These provide
information of a student’s progress in attaining their IB Diploma which requires a minimum of 24 points total and a minimum of 12 points
across HL subjects and 9 points across SL subjects.

Details of a student's attendance summary for the year to date, include percentage attendance, number of days absent and number of days late.
