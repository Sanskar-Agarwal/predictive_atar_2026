Hurlstone Agricultural High School is a NSW government fully selective, coeducational, agricultural, boarding
high school. Our school is committed to ensuring wellbeing is placed at the heart of every educational
experience we facilitate for our students. Our school nurtures the whole child in an inclusive environment
where gifted and high potential learners are encouraged to develop a passion for life-long learning and strive
to maximise their personal potential. The synergy between parents, teachers, support staff, students and
external partnerships strengthens our identity.

The school nurtures the whole child in an inclusive environment where high potential and gifted learners are
known, valued and cared for. Student voice is active and dynamic. Students give back to the community in
numerous ways including participation in the creative and performing arts, sports and agricultural programs,
and active involvement in a range of student extracurricular groups such as Rural Youth, Interact, EPIC,
Empower, Environment Committee, H Youth, Student Union, Tech Club, Chess Club, Literature and Film
Clubs, debating, mentoring and other leadership development programs highlighted by an exceptional prefect
body and Student Representative Council.

The school is strongly supported by an engaged and active Parents and Citizens' Association and Boarder
Parent Welfare Committee. Our staff provide a dynamic, creative, innovative, collaborative and differentiated
curriculum and foster connected partnerships to maximise student engagement.

Grade Statements A-E

A: Outstanding - The student has an extensive knowledge and understanding of the content and can readily
apply this knowledge. In addition, the student has achieved a very high level of competence in the processes
and skills and can apply these skills to new situations.

B: High - The student has a thorough knowledge and understanding of the content and a high level of
competence in the processes and skills. In addition, the student is able to apply this knowledge and these
skills to most situations.

C: Sound - The student has a sound knowledge and understanding of the main areas of content and has
achieved an adequate level of competence in the processes and skills.

D: Basic - The student has a basic knowledge and understanding of the content and has achieved a limited
level of competence in the processes and skills.

E: Limited - The student has an elementary knowledge and understanding in few areas of the content and
has achieved very limited competence in some of the processes and skills.

NA: A student may have been absent during the assessment task or the assessment was unable to be
completed.

Grades

A student may receive a Grade B for both the Semester 1 and Semester 2 reports and still have shown
improvement. The grades are only one component of the assessment and reporting system and the school
encourages parents to seek clarification if unsure of any issues in relation to student progress.

Cumulative Assessment Rank and Cumulative Assessment Mark

The Cumulative Assessment Rank and Cumulative Assessment Mark provide information on how a student's
academic achievement compares with the performance of the cohort. The cumulative mark is based only on
the school-based assessments included in this reporting period.
