Dhinesh Ponnappan

Dhinesh has been a student at Gosford High School for a period of 6 years and is presently a
candidate for the 2022 Higher School Certificate. During this period his attendance has been
exemplary and his pattern of study has included: Chemistry, English Advanced, Mathematics
Extension 1, Mathematics Extension 2 and Physics.

Academically, Dhinesh is an inquisitive and motivated student, and he is well organised and has
the ability and drive to succeed in any area of interest. His achievement throughout high school
was nothing short of outstanding. Dhinesh has shown himself to be a student capable of working
independently as well as able to follow directions and collaborate in group activities.

Dhinesh’s work ethic and drive to succeed is unmatched. He shows strong interpersonal skills and
actively seeks extra learning opportunities outside of the classroom in order achieve his best. He
is an outstanding role model and showed an innate ability to establish connections with others,
enhancing his own learning, as well as the learning of those around him. Dhinesh does not shy
away from opportunities to meet other people and his extroverted personality has seen him
embrace all opportunities that are presented to him. Further, his friendly and diplomatic approach
to all situations has allowed him to fulfill all social and academic responsibilities. He is dependable,
confident and self-motivated, making him an ideal leader in any environment.

Dhinesh has been an enthusiastic participant in the school sporting program and has represented
the school in Zone Cross Country. Further, he possesses strong leadership abilities that are
reflected in his participation in sporting competitions both inside and outside of the school setting.
His kind, caring and considerate nature has assured that he is well liked by both his peers and
whole school community.

Dhinesh has contributed to the school community through a variety of cultural and community
activities, including but certainly not limited to Peer Tutoring and the Elite Leadership Force (Yr 9
& 10). His commitment to these activities further demonstrates his drive and initiative to establishing
meaningful connections to assist in the development of those around him.

Dhinesh is a motivated and confident young person, who has a very promising future. He will be
an asset to any organisation and receives our highest recommendation.

ASSESSMENT OF ACHIEVEMENT AND AREAS OF LEARNING

Overall Achievement, Effort and Areas of Learning are measured using the scale ABC DE from the highest
achievement to lowest achievement.

A - Outstanding: The student has an extensive knowledge and understanding of the content and can
readily apply this knowledge. In addition, the student has achieved a very high level
of competence in the processes and skills and can apply these skills to new
situations.

B - High: The student has a thorough knowledge and understanding of the content and a high
level of competence in the processes and skills. In addition, the student is able to
apply this knowledge and these skills to most situations.

C - Sound: The student has a sound knowledge and understanding of the main areas of content
and has achieved an adequate level of competence in the processes and skills.

D - Basic: The student has a basic knowledge and understanding of the content and has
achieved a limited level of competence in the processes and skills.

E - Limited: The student has an elementary knowledge and understanding in few areas of the
content and has achieved very limited competence in some of the processes and
skills.
