Year 11 Preliminary Academic Report Guide

Central Coast Grammar School reports on a variety of student development and assessment results. This guide is provided to
help you to interpret the information provided in this academic report. Should you have any queries regarding this information
please do not hesitate to contact the school. The best person to contact in the first instance is either the subject teacher or the
Head of that Department. Each subject contains four sections:

+ Personal Work Ethic

+ Overall Rank

+ Assessment Task Standard and Rank

+ Teacher Comment

The Semester 2 reports compile the standard and rank achieved for each of the assessment tasks throughout the Preliminary
course. You will note that the tasks from Semester 1 appear on this report.

Personal Learning Profile

The Personal Learning Profile shows the student’s approach to their own learning. This profile is valuable for monitoring the
learning process. Effective study skills and work habits support long term memory and a deeper understanding of the course
content as a student progresses through their years of schooling. The result is based on the teacher’s observations and
professional judgement. The scale used in this profile is:

+ Exemplary + Improvement Required
+ Consistently Good + Significant Concern

Overall Rank

The Overall Rank for each subject describes the student's standing in relation to the subject's cohort after all assessment task
results have been combined in accordance to the specified weightings as outlined in the Assessment Guidelines.

Areas of Assessment

Standard of Achievement

Each assessment task is assessed using the standards of achievement listed below:

+ Excellent + Basic
* High + Limited
+ Sound + Unsatisfactory

Assessment Task Rank

The student's rank for individual tasks in relation to the cohort studying the subject at the time of the task is listed.

Teacher Comment

Teacher's comments are written by the class teacher. These comments give a general statement that highlights strengths and
includes possible strategies for enhancing the student's learning.

Student Profile

The student profile summarises the student’s commitment to the School Community by listing their involvement in House and
Cocurricular activities. Student attendance records are also stated. Any unexplained absences should be addressed by submitting
a written explamation signed by a parent/guardian.

Please do not hesitate to contact your child’s House Coordinator or the Head of the Department should you have
any questions regarding your child’s report.
